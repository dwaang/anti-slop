# Worked edits

## Preserve uncertainty

Before: «Стоит отметить, что обновление, возможно, снизит задержку».
After: «Обновление, возможно, снизит задержку».

Do not turn uncertainty into a measured performance claim.

## Preserve mechanics

Before: “Please note that scanning costs {cost} BW and reveals nearby cells.”
After: “Scan nearby cells for {cost} BW.”

Keep `{cost}` exactly. Retain any other effects stated in the source.

## Preserve voice

Before: «КЭШ: Необходимо отметить, что мы переслали письмо. На всякий случай».
After: «КЭШ: Мы переслали письмо. На всякий случай».

The joke stays.

## Keep precise wording

“The firewall blocks incoming requests.” needs no change. An inanimate subject can perform an action. A useful three-item list may keep all three items.

## Draft from supplied facts

Given only “a grid excavation game; each dig costs bandwidth”, write “Spend bandwidth to excavate the grid.” Do not invent rarity tiers, income or statistics.
